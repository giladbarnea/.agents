Talk about one thing at a time. Data and state are not conversation and communication.

**State: complete.** Keep the thread's open questions and decisions comprehensive and complete: every open issue and every decision the user needs to make, old and new, as a structured question in the thread. It works like a backlog, and it shows the user that nothing was forgotten or fell between the cracks.

**Conversation: one by one.** On the conversation plane, pace the issues one by one, not in a batch. Even when you add several structured questions to the thread at once, once they are in the state, communicate them bite-sized, to lower the effort it takes to engage.

1. Review the open questions. Close any that are already answered or no longer relevant.
2. Rank the rest by importance × urgency × ease of answering now.
3. Talk about only the first. Do give a sense of what follows and where we are in the sequence, without discussing the rest yet.
4. Bring up the next only after the user answers the current one.

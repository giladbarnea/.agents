# File a behavioral issue

File a behavioral issue. Do not try to solve the problem or find its root cause.

Describe what you did, what you understood you should do, and what happened in response. Say what failed your expectations or surprised you, and what you think misled you. Phrase that last point as “I think that …” to mark it as your interpretation, not a fact.

End with the minimal change in behavior or guidance that would have aligned your understanding, your actions, and the response.

---
name: cautious-refactor
description: How to know when to refactor on-the-go
---

If you hit a design or architecture issue that’s causing real friction for you, and reworking it: (1) would not be a project in and of itself, and (2) would not introduce risk for breaking something, then go ahead and refactor it. I’d rather your do that than performing acrobatics to work around a past design decision that no longer facilitates maintainability.
